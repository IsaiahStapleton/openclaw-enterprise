# Federated sign-in delivery

Deliver the [single existing-account GitHub increment](../31-human-federated-sign-in.md#implementation-and-acceptance). This page preserves earlier delivery anchors without establishing a future program.

## Increments and qualification

### Account, IAM, and State foundation

Use existing accounts, genuine Principals, passwords, and grants; connect the [original-State join](architecture.md#request-lifecycle) and [protected recovery](architecture.md#atomic-policy-and-recovery).

### Both-provider browser delivery

The earlier both-provider scope is superseded by [GitHub first](../31-human-federated-sign-in.md#first-usable-delivery). Google is tentative.

### Enterprise provider delivery

See [possible extensions](../31-human-federated-sign-in.md#selected-scope-and-exclusions); no enterprise delivery is required for this increment.

### Human CLI delivery

[CLI approval](interfaces.md#human-cli) remains deferred.

## Acceptance evidence

The [RFC acceptance contract](../31-human-federated-sign-in.md#implementation-and-acceptance) owns required proof and unresolved qualification. Documentation validation is not product evidence.

## Decisions and follow-ups

The [activation proposal](architecture.md#activation-and-supported-consumers) retains the password-account creation gap. Broader repair, joint policy, bootstrap, and runtime withdrawal retain their existing owners and [historical conditional guarantees](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw-enterprise/blob/085610f971591221b0f9c6e036e1cd7a4de89e0d/specs/31-human-federated-sign-in/delivery.md#decisions-and-follow-ups) if selected. Session denial does not prove final-byte closure, physical stop, cleanup, or resolution of unknown provider effects.
